MEGAMAX — Sparrow Mark

Logo concepts for MEGAMAX International Trading, drawn in the style of the five reference marks.

01 · What the references share

PatternWhat it looks like in the referencesHow we use it
Built from geometryEvery form is a circle, a quarter-arc or a straight cut. Nothing is drawn freehand (lime arcs + dots, the green rotating Y-forms, the black radial U-shapes).The sparrow is one quarter circle (body), one circle (head), one triangle (tail), one triangle (beak).
Negative-space cutsEven gaps split the shapes: the dot is cut from the arc, the eagle's feathers are white slits, the hawk's breast is a ring.One gap width (8 units) for every cut: head gap, two wing arcs.
Circle = head / personA solid dot stands for a head or core (lime "people", the black hub).The sparrow's head is a pure circle with a pinhole eye.
Movement & repetitionArcs and rotations imply motion and connection (exchange, rotation, radiating).Nested wing arcs read as feathers and as signals going out — reach, trade routes.
One flat colourSingle colour on a solid field; high-voltage lime or green on black/green; at most one accent (the orange star).Mono mark first. One optional chestnut accent — the house sparrow's real colour.
Compact silhouetteMarks sit in a square or circle and stay legible small. Labels in a bold geometric sans (Montserrat-style), tracked caps.Mark fits a square; wordmark in Montserrat ExtraBold with a tracked subline.

02 · Construction

Body — quarter circle, radius 100. Flat belly, upright breast, rounded back: the plump, perched shape that makes a house sparrow recognisable.

Wing — two concentric cuts at radius 65 and 35. Three feather layers that also read as outward waves: MEGAMAX shipping out in every direction.

Head — circle r26, separated by an 8-unit gap, short conical seed-eater's beak.

Tail — a straight wedge, kept low and short like a sparrow's.
