What changed

  • Geometric build. Head, body, back and chest are circles and arcs that meet tangentially. No freehand curves, so the silhouette is smooth and can be redrawn exactly.
  • One gap width. Every cut (bird outline, wing, branch) is the same 6-unit gap, like the references.
  • One branch instead of three. The circle is now in 3 large pieces instead of about 6 slivers, so it holds up at 16–32 px.
  • Clear tail. The tail is a single wedge that sits above the branch instead of merging with it and the circle edge.
  • Same character. Your deep green (#004E3C), the bird cut out of a circle, the perched pose and the wing line are kept.
  • Bolder wordmark. MEGAMAX in Montserrat ExtraBold, "INTERNATIONAL TRADING" in tracked SemiBold caps set to the same width. All text is converted to outlines.
