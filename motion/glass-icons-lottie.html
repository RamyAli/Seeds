The zip holds json/ with one Lottie JSON per icon, and lottie/ with one .lottie per icon plus glass-icons-all.lottie containing all ten. This page can save .json and .zip files but not .lottie files directly, which is why the .lottie files come inside the zip.

Each glass shape is a translucent layer over a blurred copy of the gradient, masked to the glass shape. Players that support the Gaussian Blur effect, such as lottie-web, show the frosted look; players without it show the colors sharp through the glass.
