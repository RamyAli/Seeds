What the references do — and how Lumo applies it

ImageryReference: a full-screen architectural render — low camera, monochrome concrete, warm-lit interior, grey sky — sits behind everything. Product photos on near-white.Lumo: live Three.js villa in architectural mode behind Home; battery, AC and wallbox as product renders.
ColourReference: warm greys, white circles, near-black ink; a single lime tile; orange only for the active mode and dial; charcoal nav and buttons.Lumo: same palette. Lime = the one most important tile. Orange = active mode and progress.
TypeReference: neo-grotesk, regular weight, sentence case. Titles 26, labels 15, captions 11–12. Big numerals thin. No bold anywhere.Lumo: Geist 400 for text, 300 for numerals. IBM Plex Sans Arabic for RTL.
ComponentsReference: frosted tiles over the render, preset pill, pill toggles with black knob, circle nav, mode circles, long-tick dial, wheel picker, swipe slider with lime knob.Lumo: every one of these is used, mapped to energy jobs.

Shape & spacing16 px gutters, 8 px between tiles. Radii: tiles 24, cards 22–26, sheets 38, pills = height ÷ 2. Circles 46 (header), 48 (modes), 52 (nav). Nav floats 26 px above the bottom.
MotionRender sways ±4° over 35 s; particles 0.1–0.5 loops/s by kW; toggle knob 250 ms spring; slider chevrons nudge 1.6 s; wheel numbers tick each second; coin spins and points count up over 1.2 s.
AccessibilityText ≥ 4.5:1 on paper and frost. Light 300 only at 26 px+. Targets ≥ 44 pt. Status never colour alone (dashed + "!" + word). Every canvas has a text description; reduced motion = one still frame. RTL mirrors renders, toggles and chevrons.
