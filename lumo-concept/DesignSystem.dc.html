Accessibility

Contrast. All text 4.5:1 or better. Lime is a fill, never text on white — ink on lime is 13.6:1, lime-ink #3E7C00 for small green text. Not colour alone. Every energy tile has a coloured bar and a word. Offline = dashed border + "!" + "Offline". Switches show a check. Future = dashed outline, past = hatched. Touch. All targets ≥ 44 pt: circle buttons 44, tab items 52, switches 54 × 44, chips 58 tall. 3D. Every canvas has a role="img" description of what is flowing where. Numbers always repeat in the HTML tiles, so the 3D is never the only source. Motion. Reduced motion = one still 3D frame, no counters, no confetti. RTL. The 3D scene is mirrored, layout flips with logical CSS, and arrows reverse. Numerals stay Western 0–9.
