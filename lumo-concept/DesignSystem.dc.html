Colour

Apple-style system hues, one per energy source: Solar = orange, Battery = green, Grid = blue, Home = ink. The bright fill is for shapes and lines. Text uses the deeper step, which passes 4.5:1 on glass. Colour always comes with an icon and a label.

Shape, space, touch

13 icon tiles · 22–26 chips and stats · 30–32 cards · capsules for every control (iOS 26 concentric corners)
4 pt base grid · 20 pt screen gutter · 12–14 pt between cards · floating bars sit 24 pt above the bottom edge and 16 pt in from the sides
44 Every tap target is at least 44 × 44 pt: circle buttons, switches (52 × 44), flow chips (136 × 52), tab items (≈ 80 × 52).

Accessibility

Contrast on glass. Glass is at least 56% white over a pale ground, so text is checked against the worst case (#F3F4F7). Every text pair is 4.5:1 or better. Reduce Transparency. Glass falls back to solid #FFFFFF / #1C1C1E and the ambient light switches off. Never colour alone. Every flow chip has an icon, a name and a value. Offline = dashed ring + "!" + the word. Switches show a check when on. Reduced motion. Particles stop and stay as solid lines. Ambient light stops drifting. Counters jump to their final value. RTL. The house scene mirrors, so solar and battery swap sides. Switches and progress run right-to-left. Numerals stay Western (0–9).

Voice & copy

Say what it means"Enough to run about 3 ACs" next to 5.4 kW. kWh always comes with a comparison.
Lead with the human result"82% of your home runs on sunshine".
Name every change"Pause until 7 pm · still 80% by 7 am". No hidden automation.
Reward, never guilt"Not this time", "Your streak is paused, not lost", "You keep the points earned so far".
Write for both languagesShort sentences. Arabic is written fresh, not translated word for word.
